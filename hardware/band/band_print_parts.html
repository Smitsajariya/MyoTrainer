Sensor band: print parts

One 25 mm Velcro strap carries 8 sensor pods and the electronics box. Each pod holds two FSR 402 sensors, one row near the elbow and one near the wrist, so the band has 16 FSR. Every FSR tail goes straight up into a wire groove on the outside, and the grooves lead into the box.

Print settings
Pods, buttons
TPU 95A · 0.2 mm · 3 walls · 30 % infill
Box, lid
PETG or PLA · 0.2 mm · 20 % infill
Supports
none (each part lies flat as shown)
Bed
every part fits 220 × 220 mm
Pod orientation
skin side (FSR pockets) down
Button
flat disc down, dome up
Extra parts
Strap
25 mm elastic Velcro, ≈ 34 cm
Screws
4 × M2 × 6 self-tapping (lid)
Wire
30 AWG silicone, ≈ 3 m
Glue
a drop of CA / hot glue per FSR edge
Assembly
  1. Solder two thin wires to each FSR tail, add heat-shrink.
  2. Lay the FSR in its pocket, bend the tail up through the slot, put a button on top (dome to the skin).
  3. Lay the wires in the groove; one groove per row leads to the box. Join all FSR "3V3" wires to one bus.
  4. Thread the strap through the 8 pods and the box; box on the back of the forearm.
  5. Put ESP32, multiplexer, LiPo and charger in the box, close the lid with 4 screws.
  6. Wear it ⅓ of the way from elbow to wrist, tight enough that two fingers still slide under.
Sizes come from the data sheet of the FSR 402 (Ø 18.3 mm) and an ESP32 DevKit v1. Print one pod first and check that your FSR fits before printing all eight. The script make_band.py rebuilds every STL if a size must change.
