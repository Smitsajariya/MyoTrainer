EMG + FMG sensor band

The forearm band for the virtual prosthesis trainer: 16 pressure sensors (FMG) sit inside an elastic strap around the forearm, two EMG sensors read the flexor and extensor muscles, and a wireless ESP32 on the band sends everything to the Lab app over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, powered by a small LiPo battery. Turn the model, open it up, and press a gesture to see which sensors react.

Wiring
Data lines: E,ms,emg1,emg2 every 1 ms and F,ms,f0…f15 every 10 ms, the format the Lab server already reads. The current firmware sends them over USB; for wireless use the same lines go over Bluetooth LE or Wi-Fi (firmware update needed).

Build order
  1. One FSR + 10 kΩ on the breadboard, read it on GPIO36.
  2. Add the multiplexer and all 16 FSRs.
  3. Add the two EMG sensors on GPIO34 and 35.
  4. Print the 16 pods, glue the FSRs in, thread them on the strap.
  5. Flash firmware/esp32_band and test over USB with --source serial.
  6. Add the LiPo + charger and switch the data link to Bluetooth LE: the band is wireless.
